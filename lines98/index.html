Lines 98: Pure Balls Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Lines 98: Pure Balls Puzzle does not collect any data about you.

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for or log in to.
  • No internet access. The game never connects to a network and does not ask for the permission to.
  • No ads. The game shows none and contains no in-app purchases.
  • No tracking. There are no analytics, crash reporters or advertising identifiers.

This policy applies to version 1.2.0 of the game and later (Android package com.vuzykdev.lines98), published by VuzykDev.

On your device

What the game keeps

To let you continue where you stopped, the game keeps two files in its own private app folder, which no other app can read:

  • game.save – the game in progress: the board, your score, the move history and the play time;
  • user_data.save – your settings (language, board look, sounds, music, pop-ups) and your statistics for each game mode, such as games played, best score and longest game.

None of this identifies you. It holds no name, email address, location or device identifier. The game never sends it anywhere. The "account" inside the game is only a local file with your settings and statistics. Both files are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in the system settings.

What the game does not do

  • It does not send any data to the developer or to anyone else.
  • It does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, phone, storage outside its own folder or any other device feature.
  • It does not use any third-party service for ads, analytics or sign-in.

Where other software is involved

Game engine

The game is built with the Godot Engine. The engine runs inside the game on your device and does not send data to its authors.

Rating and feedback

The settings page "Rate the game" offers two actions. Each only opens another app of your choice, and only when you tap it:

  • Write a review opens the game's page in Google Play. From that point Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Send feedback opens your mail app with a draft addressed to the developer. The subject contains the star rating you chose and the version of the game. Nothing is sent until you send the mail yourself, and your mail provider's terms apply to it. The developer uses a received mail only to answer it and to improve the game, and keeps it no longer than that takes.

The star rating you pick on that page is not stored anywhere.

App store

If you installed the game from an app store such as Google Play, the store may collect information about your download under its own privacy policy. The developer does not control that and does not receive it.

Android backup

The game allows Android's standard app-data backup. If backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may copy the two files above to your Google account together with the data of your other apps, under Google's terms and your own backup settings. The game does not control that backup and cannot read it. You can switch it off in your device's Google backup settings.

You and this policy

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal data from anyone, children included. It has no ads, no purchases and no way to contact other players.

Your data and your rights

The developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete on a server. Your saved game, settings and statistics are on your device only. You can erase them at any time by clearing the game's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game ever collects or sends any data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about the game's handling of data, can go to the developer by email: VuzykDev@proton.me

Open-source licenses
